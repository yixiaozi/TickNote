Quiet tools

Three things TickNote does well

Record on the desktop. Sync everywhere. Share only when you choose.

01

Record

A one-minute loop sits in the corner. Each round is a chance to note what you were actually doing—without opening another dashboard.

02

Sync

Work PC and laptop keep their own notes. Sign in once; they pull, push, and merge by update time so your day stays whole.

03

Share

Turn on a public page when you want. Visitors see /username/public—private by default, intentional when shared.

Start

Three steps to start

After signing up, enter the sync URL in the desktop app.

1. Create an account

Sign up free here—your username also becomes your public page path.

2. Sign in on desktop

Open TickNote → Settings → Sync, enter the server URL, and log in with the same account.

3. Go public when ready

In website Settings, turn on the public switch and share /your-username/public.
